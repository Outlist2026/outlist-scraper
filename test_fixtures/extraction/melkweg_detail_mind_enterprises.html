    The Italian band Mind Enterprises delivers a fresh mix of Italo disco, synth-pop, and electronic grooves. Producer Andrea Tirone's project started out in his kitchen in Turin but quickly grew into an international phenomenon. With their nostalgic sound, danceable beats, and striking visual style, the band manages to introduce a new generation to the sound of the 80s.

    Live, Mind Enterprises takes you to a world full of cosmic synthesizers, funky bass lines, and sultry Mediterranean disco. Their latest single, *Misty Jungle*, offers a preview of what is yet to come: an irresistible soundtrack for long nights, dance floors, and endless summer vibes. Get ready for an evening full of glitter, groove, and pure Italo disco magic.

    Support act: Shady Lady
    Shady Lady is a Sydney-born, Amsterdam-based DJ who brings together disco, house, and queer club culture in energetic and extravagant sets. Blending 70s disco, Italo, house, and rave classics, she takes her audience on a spirited journey through time, always fueled by emotion, nostalgia, and an irresistible urge to get the dance floor moving.
